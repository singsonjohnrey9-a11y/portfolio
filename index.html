WHO IS THIS GUY

article written by the zine editor (also him)

Fourth-year Information Technology student at Cebu Eastern College. Before I ever touched code, I touched engines. I sold drinking water, handled an ice cream cooler in the heat, and spent long afternoons at my uncle's shop fixing motorcycles with my hands still greasy from yesterday.

Those jobs taught me the one thing I now put into every project: keep your hands moving and fix the actual problem. That is why I like UI work, why weekend websites come together fast for me, and why my teammates appreciate that I stay calm, do my part, and keep the mood light.

My favorite anime is Serial Experiments Lain. If you know Lain, you get it. Present day, present time.
